# Home background

`assets/earth-aurora.jpg` is a downsampled NASA photograph, not a programme poster.

- Title: The aurora borealis blankets the Earth
- NASA image ID: ISS072E159172
- Created: October 30, 2024, Expedition 72 / Johnson Space Center
- Credit: NASA
- Source: https://images.nasa.gov/details/iss072e159172
- Original: https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/iss072e159172/iss072e159172~orig.jpg
- Usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The image shows the aurora above Manitoba, photographed from the International
Space Station. NASA's media entry identifies no third-party copyright holder.
NASA-produced media is generally not subject to copyright in the United States;
the linked media guidelines apply. This use does not imply NASA endorsement.

The original JPEG is downsampled to 1920 × 1280 and encoded at JPEG quality 89.
The application adds a dark readability gradient at render time and displays
the NASA credit. Installed application banners/icons are loaded from their
packages at runtime; those third-party assets are not copied into this tree.
